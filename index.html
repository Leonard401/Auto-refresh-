Privacy Policy — Auto Refresh

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Auto Refresh does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or page content.

What the extension stores

The extension saves your automation settings (mode, interval, and the CSS selector of any element you choose) locally on your device using Chrome's storage.local API. This data never leaves your browser, is not synced to any server, and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Why it requests access to all websites

The extension requests the <all_urls> host permission solely so that its refresh and auto-click features can run on whichever page you choose to activate them on. It does not read, log, or transmit page content — it only reloads pages, re-fetches the current page to update a chosen element, or simulates a click on a chosen element, all triggered locally by your own settings.

Analytics and third parties

No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used. No data is sold or shared with third parties, for any purpose.
